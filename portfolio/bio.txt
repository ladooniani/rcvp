
SELECTED EXHIBITIONS, PROJECTS, AND COLLABORATIONS, 1988–2026

1988 — Awarded a diploma for successful participation in a group exhibition at H. Akhvlediani Gallery, Georgia.

1991 — Group exhibition, H. Akhvlediani Gallery, Tbilisi, Georgia. Exhibition photographs and the September 9, 1992, issue of the Georgian Soviet magazine Drosha, featuring works from 1986–1988.
Exhibition footage (1991): https://youtu.be/HVCfUkwaX_Y
View event photos: archive.html?year=1988&cat=1988-1&img=lado-oniani-1988-2-%282%29.jpg

Some works, including the ABC series, later served as visual material for Theatre of Mimodrama posters and costume designs, including Hamlet (Georgia, 1994) and Don Juan (Germany, 1996). This artistic lineage later continued through @movementtheatre (https://www.instagram.com/movementtheatre/), alongside @synetictheatre (https://www.instagram.com/synetictheatre/).
View event photos: archive.html?year=1988&cat=1988-1&img=lado-oniani-1988-2-%281%29.jpg

1993 — Won first place in the young artists’ competition My Flying Apparatus, Georgia / France.

1994 — Design for the pantomime theatre production Hamlet, Georgia / Germany. Based on works from 1988–1991.

1995 — Exhibition, Caucasian House, Georgia.

1996 — Design for the pantomime theatre production Don Juan, Georgia / Germany. Based on works from 1988–1991.

2000 — The Sun Is My Mother, Caucasian House, Georgia.

2002 — Publication of Asymmetric Facets.

2002 — Animation, Movie House, Georgia.

2002 — Modern Art Festival, Ukraine.

2003 — Exhibition, L. Gudiashvili Exhibition Hall, Georgia.

2007 — Release of CDCTCC.

2008 — Telephone Software. A continuously operating information object that drifts through random numbers in the telephone network. If the receiver is picked up in time, after transmitting its message, the program calls the next random number.

2009 — CTP Start: TBILISI 6 — Never On Sunday. Varketili District House, Varketili-3, IV Microrayon, Building 425, Georgia, Thursday, December 3, 2009.
Project website: https://www.tbilisi6.com/
